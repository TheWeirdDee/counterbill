Tripartite resolution meeting held: 17 June 2026
Consignment: approximately 50 CBM of beach umbrellas shipped from China to Lagos.
The importer states the cargo was contracted as a dedicated 40ft full container load (FCL). On arrival it was found to have been handled as consolidated less-than-container load (LCL, groupage).
Charges faced by the importer in total: NGN 27,132,000 (about NGN 450,000 per CBM, as stated in the report).
The importer states the change from FCL to LCL happened without his authorization or knowledge.
The shipping line denied any contractual involvement. The freight forwarder produced a Master Bill of Lading and House Bill of Lading and stated it was the contracting forwarder.
The forwarder stated its overseas consolidator had been told by the factory that the goods were to be consolidated with other consignments.
The meeting concluded that a contract existed between the importer and the freight forwarder, that the cargo was processed as groupage (LCL), and that a communication breakdown about the intended mode of shipment caused the dispute.
Outcome: the importer to pay a revised flat sum of NGN 19,000,000 as full and final settlement by 25 June 2026, with proof of payment filed with the Council.
