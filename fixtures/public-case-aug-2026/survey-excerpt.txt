Complaint filed online with NPERA: 20 July 2026
Survey date: 14 August 2026
Place: cargo release facility, Ajao Estate, Lagos
An independent cargo surveyor measured each of the six outer packages in the presence of the complainant, her clearing agent and NPERA representatives.

Package dimensions recorded (length x width x height, cm):
Box 1: 120 x 34 x 22
Box 2: 83 x 56 x 60
Box 3: 67 x 46 x 34
Box 4: 50 x 24 x 50
Box 5: 109 x 38 x 40
Box 6: 182 x 64 x 5

Reported surveyed volume: 0.75 CBM
The report applies the freight rate of NGN 542,000 per CBM and describes the difference between 1.10 CBM and 0.75 CBM as an overcharge of NGN 189,700.
The report also states that the physical survey did not determine the rating methodology or the contractual terms used to calculate the 1.10 CBM chargeable volume.
The complainant paid the invoice under protest to secure release of the cargo; release was without prejudice to the freight dispute.
